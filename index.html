Professional Biography

Chatri Khatfan possesses extensive leadership and technical management experience, successfully leading technology-focused companies in both Thailand and Japan. He managed Thai Audiotex Limited and TAS Mobile Limited for three years before progressing into executive leadership roles.

After serving as a project manager at Locus Telecommunications Inc., he returned to Thai Audiotex Limited as Head of Technical Operations, overseeing technical operations and infrastructure strategy for both companies for nearly a decade.

Since 2010, Chatri has served as co-founder and Technical Director of Utel Corporation (Japan), contributing to the company's long-term technical vision, innovation strategy, and system development initiatives.

He earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Queensland University of Technology (QUT), along with postgraduate credentials in Computer System Engineering from the University of Queensland (UQ). Supported through scholarship programs, his international academic background strengthened both his technical depth and global perspective.

His expertise spans embedded systems, network architecture, software engineering, IoT systems, cloud computing, cybersecurity, AI integration, and advanced computational technologies. He is proficient in C/C++, Python, Java, system integration, and hardware troubleshooting.
